---
title: Tabs
description: Analytics, Calendar, Budgets, Tags, Flow, Trend, and List tabs in the FundsFlow web app.
---

All tabs use the period and tag filter selected at the top of the page.

## Analytics

Stat cards for the selected period, each compared with the previous period: **Income**, **Expenses**, **Net**,
**Savings rate** (share of income kept), **Transactions**, and **Avg daily expense**. Below them:

- **Forecast** — expected income, expenses, and net at the end of the period, while it is still running.
- **Insights** — short notes such as the biggest change in a tag, the priciest weekday, and no-spend days.
- **Top spending tags** with their share and change; click one to filter by it.
- **Largest expenses** of the period; **See all** opens the List.

**Balances Per Tag** shows the balance of every tag marked **Show in Balances** (including its child tags), for
**This period** or **All time**: useful for tracking things like savings or a loan.

## Calendar

- **Week / Month** — a day grid with each day's totals. Click a day to see its transactions and upcoming
  recurring payments.
- **Year** — a card per month with income, expenses, and the number of upcoming recurring payments; click a month to
  open it.

Cards at the top show actual **Income**, **Expenses**, and **Net** compared with the previous period, plus a
forecast that includes upcoming [recurring](/docs/web/recurring/) payments in the period.

## Budgets

Your [budgets](/docs/web/budgets/) with progress bars for the current period, pause/resume, edit, delete, and history.

## Tags

Income and expenses by tag, as **Donuts** or a **List**. The list shows each tag's amount and its change versus the
previous period.

Click a donut slice or a tag in the list to open its details: total, transactions, and average amount versus the
previous period, the last 12 months, subtags, and the largest transactions (**See all** opens them in the List).

## Flow

Money flow as bars of income and expenses, grouped by **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**. Cards above the chart show
**Income**, **Expenses**, **Net**, and the average expenses per day, week, or month.

Click a bar to open the List for that day, week, or month.

**Compare with previous period** (on by default) overlays the previous period on the chart and shows the change on
the cards.

:::note
Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The first and last weeks only count the days inside the selected month or year.
:::

## Trend

A line chart of your running balance over the selected period, with cards for **Net**, **Avg daily change**, the
**Low** and **Peak** (with their dates) and, while the period is running, a **Forecast** of the net at its end.
**Compare with previous period** (on by default) shows the previous period's line and the change on the cards.

## List

A table of all transactions in the period:

- **Search notes** to filter by note text.
- Sort by **Date** or **Amount** (click the column header).
- Each row shows the source icon, date, amount, tags, note, and attachments.
- Click a tag to open its details, the same view as on the Tags tab.
- Edit or hold-to-delete each row.
