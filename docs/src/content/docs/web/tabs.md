---
title: Tabs
description: Analytics, Calendar, Budgets, Tags, Flow, Trend, and List tabs in the FundsFlow web app.
---

All tabs use the period and tag filter selected at the top of the page.

## Analytics

Summary cards for the selected period, each with a percentage change versus the previous period:

- **Total Transactions**
- **Balance Change** — income minus expenses
- **Average Daily Income**
- **Average Daily Expenses**

**Balances Per Tag** shows the overall balance of every tag marked **Show in Balances** (including its child tags) —
useful for tracking things like savings or a loan.

## Calendar

- **Week / Month** — a day grid with each day's totals. Click a day to see its transactions and upcoming
  recurring payments.
- **Year** — a card per month with income, expenses, and the number of upcoming recurring payments; click a month to
  open it.

Totals at the top show actual **Income**, **Expenses**, and **Total**, plus a **Forecast** that includes upcoming
[recurring](/docs/web/recurring/) payments in the period.

## Budgets

Your [budgets](/docs/web/budgets/) with progress bars for the current period, pause/resume, edit, delete, and history.

## Tags

Income and expenses by tag, as **Donuts** or a **List**. The list shows the previous-period amount, the current amount,
and the percentage change for each tag.

## Flow

Money flow as bars of income and expenses, grouped by **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**.

Turn on **Compare with previous period** to overlay the previous period on the chart and see a table of each group
with current vs previous amounts and the difference (absolute and %).

:::note
Weekly grouping only includes full weeks inside the selected month or year; partial weeks at the edges are left out.
:::

## Trend

A line chart of your running balance over the selected period.

## List

A table of all transactions in the period:

- **Search notes** to filter by note text.
- Sort by **Date** or **Amount** (click the column header).
- Each row shows the source icon, date, amount, tags, note, and attachments.
- Edit or hold-to-delete each row.
