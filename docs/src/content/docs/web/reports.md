---
title: Reports
description: Export FundsFlow tabs as a PDF report or send it to Telegram.
---

**Settings → Report** turns the tabs you choose into a report, using the **current period type, dates, and tag filter**
from the header.

1. Choose sections: Analytics, Calendar, Budgets, Tags, Flow, Trend, List.
2. Then:
   - **Print / Save PDF** — opens your browser's print dialog, where you can save as PDF (not available inside the
     Mini App);
   - **Send to Telegram** — FundsFlow renders the report as a PDF (`fundsflow-report.pdf`) and the bot sends it to your
     linked chat.

Sending to Telegram requires a [linked Telegram account](/docs/telegram/overview/#link-telegram-to-an-existing-account).
