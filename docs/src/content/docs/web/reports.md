---
title: Reports
description: Export FundsFlow tabs as a PDF report or send it to Telegram.
---

**Settings → Report** turns the tabs you choose into a report, using the **current period type, dates, and tag filter**
from the header.

1. Choose sections: Analytics, Calendar, Budgets, Tags, Flow, Trend, List.
2. Then:
   - **Download PDF** — FundsFlow renders the report on the server and downloads the PDF (not available inside the
     Mini App);
   - **Send to Telegram** — the same PDF is sent by the bot to your linked chat.

The PDF looks like the app: it uses your theme (light or dark) and the same charts as the tabs. The assistant's report
card and the Telegram assistant's reports produce the same PDF.

Sending to Telegram requires a [linked Telegram account](/docs/telegram/overview/#link-telegram-to-an-existing-account).
