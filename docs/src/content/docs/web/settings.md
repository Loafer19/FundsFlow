Open **Settings** (⚙️) in the header. It opens as a side panel with five sections.

## Appearance

- **Date** format, e.g. `YYYY/MM/DD`, `MM/DD/YYYY`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `DD.MM.YYYY`, `DD.MM`, or long/short month names.
- **Money** format: `1.234,56`, `1 234,56`, `1,234.56`, `1'234.56`, or `12,34,567.89`, and **Show decimals**.
- **Timezone** — used for “today”, dates of transactions you log in Telegram, and the daily AI limit reset.
- **Theme** — pick one of the built-in color themes (the default is *Bumblebee*). Star themes to keep them in
  **Favorites** at the top.

Click **Save** to apply.

## Account

- **AI assists today** — used / limit and the reset time for the [daily AI limit](/docs/telegram/ai-assistant/#daily-ai-limit), shared
  by the Telegram bot and the web [AI assistant](/docs/web/assistant/).
- **Login credentials** — set an email and password, so you can log in without Telegram, Google, or GitHub. This is also
  what MCP OAuth sign-in uses.
- **Google**, **GitHub** — linked status.

## Connections

- **Telegram** — linked status. **Open Bot** links your chat
  ([details](/docs/telegram/overview/#link-telegram-to-an-existing-account)).
- **MCP** — generate a personal token for AI clients. See [MCP](/docs/ai/connect-clients/#personal-token).
- **Monobank** — connect a personal token so new transactions are imported automatically. Only UAH cards
  are synced; transfers between your own cards and jars are not counted; pending purchases appear right
  away and update when they settle; jars can be tracked as savings goals. See
  [Monobank sync](/docs/help/monobank/).

## Data & reports

- **Manage Tags** and **Manage Types** — open the [tags](/docs/web/tags/) and [transaction types](/docs/web/types/)
  editors.
- **Import statement** — opens the [import page](/docs/web/import/) to check a bank file before anything is saved.
- **Report** — pick the sections and **Download PDF** or **Send to Telegram**. See [Reports](/docs/web/reports/).
- **Backup** — **Download JSON** exports all your data in one file: account, tags, transactions (with attachment
  metadata), budgets, recurring rules, and transaction types. Not available inside the Telegram Mini App.

## Support

Your requests to the FundsFlow team and our replies. Click a request to read the conversation and answer; answering
a closed request opens it again. **New request** opens the same form as **Feedback** at the bottom of the page.
Replies also come to Telegram when it's linked. See the [FAQ](/docs/help/faq/#how-do-i-report-a-problem-or-send-feedback).
